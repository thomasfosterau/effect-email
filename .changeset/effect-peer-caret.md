The `effect` peer range is now `^4.0.0` (was `>=4.0.0-rc.118`), per the peer-range policy: every 4.x release from 4.0.0 is supported, and a package's floor is raised only when one of its own dependencies requires it. Only the form of the range changes; Effect 4 release candidates are no longer in range.
